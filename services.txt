If you're building a marketing and digital agency, I recommend offering **5 core service categories**. This will make your agency look professional, keep your services organized, and help potential clients understand exactly what you do.

Here is the service list I would use for your agency.

# 01. Brand Identity & Strategy
- Brand Strategy & Positioning
- Logo & Visual Identity Design
- Brand Guidelines
- Brand Messaging & Voice
- Packaging Design
- Brand Refresh & Rebranding
- Pitch Deck Design

# 02. Website Design & Development
- Website Design & Development
- E-commerce Website Development
- Landing Page Design
- Website Redesign
- UI/UX Design
- Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)
- Website Maintenance
- Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

# 03. Marketing & Growth
- Digital Marketing Strategy
- Social Media Marketing
- Paid Advertising (Meta & Google Ads)
- Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
- Content Marketing
- Email Marketing & Automation
- Copywriting
- Lead Generation
- Campaign Management
- Analytics & Performance Reporting

# 04. AI & Business Automation
- AI Chatbots & Customer Support
- AI Workflow Automation
- Lead Qualification & Follow-ups

- Business Process Automation

# 05. Creative & Motion Design

## What I Would Prioritize

Don't try to sell all these services equally from day one.

| Priority | Category | Main objective |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing & Growth | Generate recurring monthly revenue |
| 2 | Website Design & Development | Win high-value projects |
| 3 | AI & Business Automation | Differentiate your agency |
| 4 | Brand Identity & Strategy | Build brands and attract new businesses |
| 5 | Creative & Motion Design | Support campaigns and generate additional revenue |
| 6 | Digital Product Design | Target technology companies and startups |

### Three important decisions

- **Add paid advertising.** Explicitly list Meta Ads and Google Ads rather than using the vague term "campaigns."
- **Keep SEO under Marketing & Growth.** You can mention it under Websites as well, but avoid presenting it as two separate services.
- **Sell AI solutions based on business outcomes.** For example, automated lead follow-ups are clearer and easier to sell than technical terms such as agentic orchestration.

### How I would present the categories on your website

For the clean, minimal layout in your reference images, I would use these five headings:

**(01) Brand Identity**

**(02) Websites & Digital Products**

**(03) Marketing & Growth**

**(04) AI & Automation**

**(05) Creative & Motion**

This gives your agency a broad range of capabilities while keeping the presentation simple and modern.

**My next recommendation:** Start by choosing your target clients—such as SaaS startups, e-commerce brands, or established businesses—because that decision will determine which of these services you should emphasize and which you should leave out initially.
